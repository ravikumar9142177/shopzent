# Limitations — please read

* **Payments are manual.** There is no payment gateway: the customer pays by UPI and submits the UTR; an admin verifies it in the bank/UPI app. No automatic payment confirmation, no auto-refunds. Orders wait until you verify.
* **Amount-filled QR:** the payment page builds a standard UPI QR (`upi://pay?pa=techravi@fam&pn=Ravi Kumar&am=<amount>&cu=INR&tn=<Order ID>`) from your UPI ID. It is a genuine QR for your own ID, but some UPI apps restrict or warn about prefilled amounts for personal (non-merchant) IDs. Your own uploaded QR (no amount) is shown as the fallback, and the customer types the amount. A UPI **merchant** account (e.g. from your bank or a payments app) removes most of these limits.
* **WhatsApp is click-to-chat only.** A website cannot silently send a WhatsApp message or an image — that needs the paid WhatsApp Business API, which is not set up. What happens instead: when the customer submits the payment form, their own WhatsApp opens with everything filled in (name, Customer ID, Order ID, product, amount, UTR, screenshot link) to 9942598154, and **they tap Send**. Some phones block the automatic opening; the big "Send on WhatsApp" button on the success screen does the same. The screenshot image itself must be attached by the customer in WhatsApp. With Supabase connected the message carries a private 7-day link to the screenshot (only the customer who uploaded it and admins can create that link; anyone who has the link can open it until it expires). In demo mode there is no storage, so the message says "attached in this chat". Either way the UTR and screenshot are always in Admin → Payments.
* **Affiliate products:** prices/ratings are not fetched from Amazon/Flipkart automatically — you enter them and the "price last verified" date. Prices on Amazon/Flipkart change; the final price is the marketplace's. You must use **your own** Amazon Associates / Flipkart Affiliate IDs and follow their rules (disclosure page included). Affiliate clicks are counted on SHOPZENT; commissions are only visible in your affiliate dashboards.
* **Coins are not money.** 5,000,000,000 is an internal treasury figure for promotions. Coins cannot be withdrawn, sold or converted to rupees, and do not reduce order totals unless you add such a rule later. Store credit (wallet) is rupee credit usable only on SHOPZENT, not withdrawable.
* **GitHub Pages:** static hosting only, no custom HTTP headers (see SECURITY.md for the Cloudflare fix), soft limits of 1 GB site size and 100 GB/month bandwidth.
* **Supabase free plan:** 500 MB database, 1 GB storage, 50 MB per file upload limit, limited emails per hour without your own SMTP, project pauses after ~7 days of no activity, no downloadable automatic backups. Upgrade to Pro when orders grow.
* **APKs:** only distribute apps you own or have permission to share. Without a VirusTotal key, APKs stay "not verified" and are **not downloadable** (by design). Large APKs over the free plan's upload limit need Pro or an external official link.
* **Search** is simple text search (name/description), not typo-tolerant.
* **No delivery partner integration:** shipping status and tracking numbers are entered by the admin.
* **Demo mode** (no Supabase keys) keeps data only in the visitor's own browser — it is for preview only. Registrations, orders and the admin panel's data are not shared between phones. The demo admin login checks a PBKDF2 hash that is inside the public website files; it is not a real security boundary (anyone can read the demo files, and demo data is local anyway). Real protection starts when Supabase is connected — the deploy then removes that hash.
* **Coupon codes in the package:** the website files contain only SHA-256 fingerprints of the codes (plus a scrambled copy for the demo admin list), never the codes in plain text. That is obfuscation, not encryption: short codes can be guessed from a fingerprint by someone determined. The database seed writes the starting codes base64-encoded, which anyone can decode if the GitHub repository is public. If that matters, rename the codes in Admin → Coupons after setup. With Supabase connected, codes live only in the database (admin-only) and are checked one typed code at a time.
* **Google login** works only after Supabase is connected and the Google provider is enabled (SETUP.md). In demo mode the button only explains this.
* **Custom domain:** the website cannot attach a domain to itself. Admin → Domain shows the DNS records and checks them through Google public DNS; the final switch is in GitHub → Settings → Pages.
* **Legal pages** are general templates, not legal advice. Have them reviewed (GST, consumer protection, IT rules) for your business.
* **Sample content:** sample products/brands are labelled "(sample)" and exist only in demo mode. The real database starts with no products — add genuine ones with genuine photos.
