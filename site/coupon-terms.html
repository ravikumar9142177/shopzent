Coupon Terms

Last updated: 4 November 2026

  • Coupons apply to eligible SHOPZENT direct purchases only, not to affiliate (Amazon/Flipkart) products.
  • Each coupon may have a start date, expiry date, minimum order value, maximum discount, total usage limit and per-customer limit.
  • Some products, brands or categories may be excluded. A product may also carry its own discount; whether a coupon combines with it is set per product.
  • Only one coupon per order unless a coupon explicitly allows combining.
  • Discounts are calculated by our server at checkout; the amount shown in your order is final.
  • If an order using a coupon is cancelled before payment verification, the coupon use is released.
  • Coupons have no cash value and cannot be exchanged. SHOPZENT may cancel coupons used fraudulently.
