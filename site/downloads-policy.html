Digital Downloads Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

  • SHOPZENT lists apps and digital files only where we have the right to distribute them, or link to the official developer/store page. Third-party apps belong to their developers and are marked "Third-party".
  • Verification status is honest: an app shows "Scanned" only after its file has actually been scanned. Files that are not verified show "Not verified", and uploaded APK files are kept private and are not downloadable until they pass a scan.
  • Installing APK files from outside the Play Store carries risk. Check the app's permissions, keep your phone updated, and turn off "Install unknown apps" afterwards.
  • Report a harmful file or a copyright problem to ravitechsolution.shop@gmail.com and we will review and remove it promptly.
  • Digital downloads are generally not returnable once delivered, unless the file is faulty.
