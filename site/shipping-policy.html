Shipping Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

  • We ship SHOPZENT direct products across India where our courier partners deliver.
  • Dispatch: usually within 2–3 working days after your payment is verified.
  • Delivery time: typically 4–8 working days depending on your pincode. Estimates are not guarantees.
  • Charges: any delivery charge is shown at checkout before you pay.
  • Tracking: your order page shows the status as soon as we update it. We mark an order "Shipped" or "Delivered" only when that has actually happened.
  • Failed delivery: if a parcel returns because of a wrong address or the receiver is unavailable, we will contact you; re-shipping charges may apply.
  • Affiliate products are shipped by the marketplace you bought from.
