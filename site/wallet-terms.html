Wallet & Coins Terms

Last updated: 4 November 2026

SHOPZENT Wallet (store credit)

  • Every registered customer gets a unique Customer ID (SZC-…) and Wallet ID (SZW-…).
  • Wallet balance is store credit issued by SHOPZENT (for example approved return credits or promotional credits). It can be used only for eligible purchases on SHOPZENT.
  • It is not a bank account, deposit, prepaid payment instrument or money-transfer service. It cannot be withdrawn as cash, transferred to other people, or topped up with money.
  • Every credit and debit is recorded in a permanent ledger with a reference number. Mistakes are corrected only through a recorded reversal.
  • Refunds are paid to your original payment method by default. Store credit is used for a refund only when you choose and accept it and the law allows.

SHOPZENT Coins (loyalty points)

  • Coins are promotional loyalty points. At checkout, 1 coin is worth ₹0.80 (the rate shown at checkout applies). Coins have no cash value, are not money and cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn. Coins cannot be sent to other customers.
  • Coins are issued at SHOPZENT's discretion and may expire as stated when issued. SHOPZENT may change or end the coin programme with notice.
  • The internal coin supply shown in our systems is an accounting figure for promotions, not money held by anyone.
